Delete Your JK MARTS Account

If you want to delete your JK MARTS account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

Steps to Request Account Deletion

  1. Email us from your registered email address.
  2. Send your request to: jkmarts.in@gmail.com
  3. Include your registered phone number.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Delivery addresses
  • Order history

Your data will be deleted within 7–30 days after verification.

If you have any questions, contact us at jkmarts.in@gmail.com.
